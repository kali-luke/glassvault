Prototype tools

Throwaway prototype. Assumptions to check: 5 locks, 1:30 per lock, 3 tries, hints cost 10 seconds. Running out of time or tries forces the lock open, same as a skip. Days before 23 Oct are example heists. Yesterday is free; older days need Premium (nothing is charged in the prototype).

Ranking idea: most locks cracked first, then fastest time, so skipping hard locks never pays.
